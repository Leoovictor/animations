# O Grande Cisma de 1054 — Comic History Cartoon (vertical, TikTok)

**Idea:** the Church as a printed map of paper cut in half; two paper puppets personify the two Churches (Rome and Constantinople) — never real people. 131 s, pt-BR voice, loop: the end puts the map back together and lands on the "1054" of the opening.

Candidate structures: (1) a straight chronicle 1054 → 1965; (2) a mystery ("one word", "a dead pope") opened in the hook and paid off along the way; (3) a "three fights" countdown. **Chosen: 2 + 3** — the hook plants two questions (the word F______E, the dead pope) and a promise (900 years); the middle is three numbered fights (BRIGA Nº 1–3) and pays off "Filioque"; 1204 is the only serious, dark panel; 1965 tears up the excommunications; the ending asks "you knew they were once one Church?", shows the comment sticker, and returns to the loop.

Scenes: hook · mapa · quem manda · filioque · pão · bula (Hagia Sophia, 16 Jul 1054) · Leão IX's empty chair · slow drifting apart · 1204 · 1965 · today · return.
Rules: no depiction of God; no real person depicted (popes and patriarchs are only named in the narration); Hagia Sophia without minarets (it was 1054); the map is schematic (FACTS.md).
